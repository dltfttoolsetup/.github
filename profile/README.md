Understanding FRP Lock
Factory Reset Protection (FRP) is a security feature introduced with Android 5.1 (Lollipop). It helps protect personal data by requiring the Google account previously linked to the device after a factory reset.

FRP can become a problem if:

You forget the Google account password.
You purchase a second-hand phone that still has the previous owner's Google account linked.
A device is reset without first removing the associated Google account.
On supported devices, TFT Unlock Tool can assist with FRP-related service operations. However, it should only be used on devices that you own or have permission to repair.
